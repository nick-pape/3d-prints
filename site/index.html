Power bank station generator

A desk charging station with a slot per power bank and a built-in cable box. Pick the bank and the number of slots, preview it, download STL or a PrusaSlicer 3MF. Everything runs in your browser.

The 3MF has the station and your inserts laid out on the bed, with the settings it was designed with: Prusa MK4, 0.6 mm HF nozzle, Prusament PETG, 0.40 mm layers, no supports. Use the STLs for other printers. Print the test-fit slices from the repo first if you're unsure about clearance.
